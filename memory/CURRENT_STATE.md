# Current project state

## Objective

Research a local-first, model-agnostic long-term memory layer for LLM agents. The model context window remains working memory; durable evidence, experiences, conclusions, and procedures live on the user's disk.

## Current phase

Evidence collection, comparative biological-memory expansion, interdisciplinary mechanism mapping, source auditing, candidate preservation, and formal laboratory design. Broad discovery now runs alongside reversible screening/synthesis cycles. We are not yet claiming a validated final architecture. Mechanisms advance through registered stage gates rather than plausibility alone.

## Project-memory bootstrap

A dependency-free local memory now supports continuity of this research:

- Git-tracked append-only JSONL and reviewed Markdown are canonical;
- SQLite FTS5 is a disposable local index;
- MCP stdio serves Codex, Claude Code, and other clients;
- CLI remains available to clients without MCP;
- no model API key, vector database, graph database, or cloud account is required.
- external API models are optional, replaceable batch workers; they may write review candidates but never accepted evidence directly.

## Latest diagnostics (2026-08-22)

- A budgeted DeepSeek V4 Flash worker screened literature, challenged methodology, and served as a synthetic reader. Reader v0-v4 exposed successive gold, chronology, cross-case, and condition-name leakage channels. Fully audited v5 cost USD 0.05218136. Cumulative conservative cost including invalid runs is USD 0.32598016. The worker remains optional and replaceable.
- The 37 normalized `include` candidates passed bibliographic identity resolution, but represented 35 distinct works after merging one version duplicate and one peer-review artifact. Abstract screening assigned 19 priority full reads, 9 background sources, 6 deferred sources, and 1 challenge-only preprint.
- A 24-query PMLAB development slice compared no memory, ripgrep, and SQLite FTS5. The lexical backends reached Recall@5 0.841 but showed forbidden-record intrusion 0.292, no successful abstention, and zero cross-language recall. This is an authored instrument test, not architecture evidence.
- The interference branch now separates capture, storage integrity, access, selection/context, reader use, and final action failures. Five preregistration drafts cover fault localization, update interference, reversible forgetting, cue-dependent recovery integrity, and retrieval-neighbor competition; none is yet a frozen confirmatory benchmark.
- The deterministic F1/F2 development run contains 28 authored fault traces, 256 versioned records, and 56 queries. F1 passed its own stage logic but is not independent. In the templated F2 fixture, `rg` and FTS5 current-value AUC was 0.417 with stale intrusion 0.857; an exact-entity/ISO-date validity rule scored 1.0 but is template-fitted and not held out.
- An unseen-template challenge added 16 multi-fault or missing-telemetry F1 traces and 154 records with 24 F2 queries. The B3 rule resolver fell to Recall@5 0.50, scored zero on natural-language and relative time, and abstained correctly on only 0.25 of unanswerable cases. Its sufficiency is rejected; entity resolution, temporal normalization, and validity filtering must be tested separately.
- Oracle query normalization raised challenge FTS5 Recall@5 from 0.40 to 1.00, while oracle history scope added 0.00; forbidden-version intrusion stayed 1.00. In reader stress v5, full/weak cues and explicit abstention policy were perfect; minimal policy scored 8/16 with absent cues and 4/16 with contradictory current markers. Record count alone is not the supported mechanism.
- Across 174 authored fault traces, passive cascading telemetry reached exact fault-set accuracy 0.075, root accuracy 0.511, and physical-data-loss decision coverage 0.414. Ideal isolated active probes scored 1.0 by construction. This establishes an observability requirement, not real probe accuracy; probe noise and budget must be tested next.
- A 1,972-case active-probe robustness matrix showed that repeat-all reached exact fault localization 0.794 at 30 probe units, while adaptive failure-domain-diverse storage checks reached 0.741 at 24.16 units. The latter made zero false loss/no-loss declarations with 0.909 coverage. Persistent and correlated failures defeated same-probe repetition; the F1 0.95 promotion threshold remains unpassed.
- Under an exact 580-contribution transient-flip mixture, reaching 0.95 exact localization requires auditing 72.38% of first-pass healthy non-storage probes, for 28.31 expected units versus 30 for repeat-all. Cheap failure-only retry is therefore unsupported without empirical, probe-specific false-health priors.
- Probe dependency map v0 shows that direct-ID, full-scan, raw-byte, and schema probes all share the primary filesystem/device. They can distinguish parser/index access failures but cannot alone confirm physical loss. A separately inventoried alternate-media probe and empirical injection harness are now explicit prerequisites.
- The disposable real-file harness exercised 12 injection classes over 300 same-device trials and matched all authored outcomes while emitting zero `PHYSICAL_LOSS_CONFIRMED` decisions. This validates executable semantics only. Windows maps both C: and D: to Disk 0, so P10 alternate-media evidence and empirical reliability estimates remain unavailable.
- A targeted metamemory/selective-control pass separates monitoring from control and rejects confidence as privileged trace access. Human FOK can follow cue familiarity or accessible partial/incorrect information; LLM semantic/sample agreement is reader-level evidence that can share common-mode error. A typed metamemory-control benchmark now has cases, arms, metrics, and candidate gates.
- The 26-case metamemory-control development corpus was frozen before runner implementation at commit `a4d5d3c`. On its authored transitions, typed monitoring alone was safe but recovered 0.333 of answerable stored targets; typed control recovered 1.0 with zero selective risk at 0.692 coverage and 0.981 abstract cost. All three scalar arms had 0.8 risk at 0.385 coverage because the corpus deliberately includes high-confidence common-mode errors. This validates construction semantics only, not generalization.
- A post-hoc rerun on the earlier 24-query PMLAB corpus found that `rg` and FTS5 were jointly unsafe on 13 cases and remained unsafe on 11/19 cases where top-1 agreed. An agreement gate at Jaccard >=0.8 plus top-1 match reduced coverage to 0.542 but left selective retrieval risk at 0.538. Same-cue lexical agreement is rejected as independent confidence evidence; the next monitor test needs temporal, entity, or bilingual cue diversity.
- The diverse-cue runner and gates were frozen at `43cdec0` before execution. Valid-time, trust, and tailored bilingual interventions raised safe-action accuracy from 0.458 to 0.833, reduced selective retrieval risk from 0.522 to 0.167, eliminated forbidden intrusion, and recovered 2/2 cross-language targets. The bundle is rejected because unanswerable abstention remained 0/2. The four residual errors were weak paraphrase, incomplete multi-source contradiction, and two absent facts; evidence sufficiency/completeness must be isolated next.
- A targeted answerability/RAG-evaluation pass separates relevance, validity, answerability, facet and bridge completeness, claim support, citation completeness, conflict resolution, and collection closure. SQuAD 2.0, ASQA, ALCE, ARES, RAGChecker, RAGTruth, coverage-error work, and Self-RAG supply benchmark mechanisms but no privileged sufficiency oracle. PMLAB-SUFF-001 now proposes typed obligations and explicit `partial_with_gap` actions.
- PMLAB-SUFF-001 froze 36 authored PL/EN cases at `4ca0309` before runner implementation. Non-empty retrieval risk was 0.75; four scalar arms scored 0.80; claim entailment scored 0.20 with two critical false-sufficient cases. Retrieved-obligation monitoring reached 0.778 exact action and zero answer risk but could not distinguish continued search from collection-confirmed absence/partiality. The gold collection hybrid reproduced the contract, while matched-coverage gates failed. Real decomposition, mapping, retrieval, and independent labels remain required.

V1 repaired only that isolation issue and froze at `e9649ac`. It was authored after template inspection and is not held out. Stale intrusion remained 1.0.
