Third-party paper abstracts (not relicensed)

Some files in this repository reproduce the plain-text abstract of published papers next to
bibliographic metadata taken from OpenAlex. OpenAlex releases its metadata under CC0, but it
distributes abstracts only as word-position indexes and does not grant rights in the abstract
wording. The wording of each abstract remains the property of its authors or publisher and is
NOT licensed to you by this repository.

What this repository does license (CC BY 4.0): its own selection, arrangement, queries, scores
and annotations around those abstracts.

What you may do: read the abstracts for research evaluation; follow `landing_page`, `doi` or
`openalex_id` to the original; regenerate the abstract text yourself from the OpenAlex API.
For any other use, obtain permission from the rights holder of the paper concerned.

Removal: if you are a rights holder and want an abstract removed, open an issue in this
repository and it will be removed from the current tree promptly.
