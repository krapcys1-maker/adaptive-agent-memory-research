# Audit disposition

Status: completed author disposition of an M1 model advisory  
Audit verdict: `needs_revision` (retained; repairs do not retroactively change it)  
Authority: transparency repair only; no independent validation or architecture promotion

## Accepted findings and repairs

| Audit concern | Disposition | Repair |
|---|---|---|
| The review packet did not expose per-query failure evidence. | Accepted. The committed execution already contained per-query retrieval and pack rows, but the reviewer packet made the claims unnecessarily hard to verify. | Added deterministic `failure-analysis.json`, generated by `scripts/analyze_reuse_characterization.py`, containing query text, ranked IDs, missing IDs, forbidden IDs, and candidate lists for the unanswerable query. |
| RRF/dense forbidden overlap was not shown. | Accepted as a reporting gap. | The derived analysis reports exact sets and proves that RRF `{Q04,Q06,Q07}` is a subset of dense `{Q04,Q06,Q07,Q08}`. The experiment report now calls this descriptive overlap, not causal inheritance. |
| Per-arm pack metrics could not be recovered from the aggregate summary alone. | Accepted. | The derived analysis reports required retention, bytes, omissions, and required-evidence loss per arm and mode. |
| Warm timings were not included in the aggregate artifact. | Accepted. | Added `warm-run-receipt.json` with the second fresh-process values and labelled them descriptive warm-cache timings, not a controlled performance benchmark. |
| `Admit` could imply validation of evidence truth or bucket inference. | Accepted. | Replaced it with narrower language: deterministic implementations advance only to later tests; truth, classification, safety, and reader behavior remain unvalidated. |
| Future hypotheses and test ideas needed an explicit post-hoc label. | Accepted. | Both the result and follow-up now state that these are post-hoc proposals requiring a separately frozen protocol before execution. |

## Rejected or narrowed findings

| Audit claim | Disposition | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Per-query results do not exist. | Rejected as a statement about the repository; accepted as a packet-scope criticism. | `retrieval-results.jsonl` and `pack-results.jsonl` were committed before the review. `failure-analysis.json` makes them directly inspectable. |
| Candidate-null `0` does not prove candidates were returned. | Rejected after localization. | The derived artifact lists five returned IDs for Q18 for every arm. This still does not establish semantic abstention or answerability. |
| Dense bucket retention conflicts with the aggregate bucket mean. | Rejected as a contradiction; accepted as insufficiently explained aggregation. | The aggregate combines all arms. Per-arm raw/bucketed dense retention is identical at `0.982456`; FTS5 and RRF account for the aggregate reduction. |
| Merely proposing a future test outside the frozen protocol is a process violation. | Rejected. | Executing or interpreting an unregistered test as part of the frozen experiment would violate the protocol. Clearly labelled post-hoc proposals are preserved as hypotheses and require new preregistration before execution. |
| Retaining arms as future comparators violates the lack of an architecture gate. | Narrowed. | Comparator preservation does not select an architecture. The report now explicitly limits advancement to later testing and keeps all retrieval choices unresolved. |

## Residual limitations

- The reviewer was invoked by the author, saw an author-selected packet, and is from the same model family used elsewhere in screening; it is not independent review.
- Failure localization is post-hoc and descriptive. It cannot support retuning `k`, depth, weights, thresholds, or the fixture.
- The authored labels and buckets were not independently assessed.
- No reader model consumed the packs, so useful ordering, evidence use, injection resistance, and answer quality remain unknown.
- The locked natural-history benchmark remains blocked on independent corpus eligibility and evidence labels.

## Decision

Keep the audit verdict `needs_revision` as provenance. The accepted transparency repairs are complete, but no claim is promoted. The synthetic fixture is spent; any compact-citation, ordering, trust-filter, completeness-controller, E5, or fusion experiment requires its own frozen protocol and non-visible evaluation data.
