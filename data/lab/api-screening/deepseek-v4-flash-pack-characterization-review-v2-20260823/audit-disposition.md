# Audit disposition

Status: completed author disposition of an M1 model advisory  
Verdict retained: `accept_characterization_with_limits`  
Authority: not independent validation and no architecture promotion

## Accepted findings

- A fresh answer-bearing reader protocol, blinded condition labels, exact scoring, and equal evidence IDs are required before testing comprehension or citation emission.
- The visible 24-case fixture supports no natural-transfer or population-generalization claim.
- Required-source reuse is a registered case-level stratum, not a causal isolation of every candidate-path reuse mechanism.
- Greedy first-fit is one packer and later sensitivity work should compare alternatives on new data.
- UTF-8 capacity is provider-neutral serialization evidence, not provider token or monetary cost.
- `O2_REQUIRED_ORACLE` is privileged and must be excluded from deployable reader comparisons.

## Corrected or narrowed findings

### Oracle aggregation did not inflate the format delta

The audit says including `O2_REQUIRED_ORACLE` inflated the primary 768-byte compact-minus-full delta. This direction is incorrect. Both formats retained `1.0` under oracle order, so including it diluted the difference:

- retrieval order: compact `0.549` minus full `0.438` = `+0.111`;
- governed order: compact `0.819` minus full `0.667` = `+0.153`;
- non-oracle mean difference: `+0.132`;
- registered all-order mean difference including the zero oracle contrast: `+0.088`.

The recommendation to exclude oracle from deployable comparisons is accepted. The frozen registered calculation remains unchanged for provenance; the non-oracle value is labelled descriptive post-hoc.

### The numeric gate does not claim reader readiness

The report already states that the gate authorizes only drafting and freezing a reader protocol, not an API call or a comprehension claim. To reduce ambiguity, subsequent documents call it the `reader-protocol drafting gate`.

### No uncertainty interval is required for a full deterministic fixture description

The absence of uncertainty is retained as a generalization limitation. We do not treat the 24 authored cases as a random sample from a defined population, so an interval would not create external validity. Future independently sampled or grouped reader data must preregister its uncertainty analysis.
