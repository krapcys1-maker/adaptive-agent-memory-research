E6 and the compatible H2 subsets show different trade-offs; H2-valid gains at depth 3 but loses at depth 10. Most importantly, the shipped live evidence floor rejects every positive H2 query even though opening the gate improves its ranking at depth 3. Ranking superiority is not gate utility.

The cross-domain negative bank produced no firing in the recorded sweeps. That bank therefore does not discriminate false-positive behavior in this setting and is not evidence of real-world precision. Near-domain independently labelled no-answer probes remain useful follow-up work. No gate threshold was relaxed after this observation.
