# AAM-E1-DS: the AAM-E1-SIM design replicated with a strong writer on fresh streams

**Status: exploratory, tier E, not entered in the experiment registry; preregistered before the first record.** Everything about the question, the arms, the stream, the measures, the analysis, the practical threshold and the readings is exactly what `data/lab/aam-e1-writer-memory-sim/README.md` states, addenda A to C included. This file lists only what differs. The scripts are the same files, frozen by the SHA-256 digests in `config.json`, and nothing in them was changed for this run.

## What differs from AAM-E1-SIM

- **Writer.** `deepseek-flash`, the provider's name for its flash model, reached through the local metering proxy (`scripts/metered_proxy.py`) in non-thinking mode (the request field `thinking: {"type": "disabled"}` is added by the proxy; temperature 0.8, top-p 0.95 and at most 320 tokens are set by the harness, as in E1). The provider ignores the `seed` field, so the entries cannot be regenerated by re-running; the stored records are the data, and the analysis of them is deterministic. The trace of M3a and M3b is written by the same model at temperature 0.
- **Streams.** Stream seeds 4 to 9: six fresh streams of 36 days, so 180 analysis entries per arm (E1 had three streams and 90).
- **Judges.** The blind judge of E1's addendum A is run twice: `qwen2.5:14b-instruct` locally (a different family from the writer, so the cross-family judge here and the primary one) and `deepseek-v4-pro` through the proxy (the same family as the writer, so the secondary one, with a self-preference risk that is the same for every arm). Same prompt, same planted controls, same rule that a dimension is informative only if the judge notices its controls, same quality guard. If both judges are informative on a dimension and disagree in the sign of a contrast, the contrast is reported as unresolved.
- **Budget.** All paid-API work of this phase is capped at 12 USD (the owner's budget is 15 USD) by the proxy and its ledger, `data/lab/aam-deepseek-2026-11/spend-ledger.jsonl`, which holds no message content. The cost is computed at the arena's list price, which is deliberately high; the account balance read from the provider before and after the run is recorded in `run-meta.json` as an independent check.

## What counts as a replication

A reading of E1 (raw history helps, raw history hurts, traces beat entries, dynamics add, nothing moves) is **replicated** if E1-DS gives the same reading by the same rule, with the quality guard holding; it is **not replicated** if E1-DS gives a different reading or none; if E1 and E1-DS point the same way but only one passes the threshold, the result is **mixed** and is reported as mixed. E1-DS has twice the entries and a different model, so a difference between the two is information about the writer as much as about noise, and is described as such. Thresholds are not retuned after either run.

## Limits

One provider's model, one persona, six themes, six streams. The provider's model and its serving can change between the day of the run and any later attempt to reproduce it. Cost at the list price over-counts. Everything in the limits of E1 applies.

## Results (written on 2026-11-01 after the preregistered analysis had been run)

**The run.** 1,080 entries (6 streams x 36 days x 5 arms), `deepseek-flash` in non-thinking mode, 14 minutes; 1,512 calls including the traces, 650k prompt and 216k completion tokens, 0.41 USD at the arena's list price (`proxy-summary.json`). The harness digest in the run metadata equals the one frozen in `config.json`. The provider balance read before and after the run (`balance-before.json`, 24.14 USD, and 23.27 USD read afterwards) also covers judge calls made for other experiments in the same interval, so it is a check on the order of magnitude and not a per-run figure. Analysis: `report.md`, `results.json`.

**The readings stated in advance: "nothing moves" holds** (every difference in overlap4 is below the practical threshold of 0.05; the other four readings do not fire). As the independent review of AAM-E1-SIM pointed out, the threshold exceeds the level of overlap4 in arm M0 (0.049 here), so no reduction reading could fire; the effects are read descriptively.

| Arm | overlap4 | explicit references back | words | judged fit / fresh / quality (same-family judge) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| M0 none | 0.049 | 1.1% | 133 | 4.99 / 4.06 / 4.78 |
| M1 last three entries | 0.045 | 3.9% | 139 | 5.00 / 4.15 / 4.88 |
| M2 raw retrieval | 0.034 | 5.0% | 139 | 5.00 / 4.13 / 4.93 |
| M3a traces | 0.040 | 8.9% | 137 | 5.00 / 4.06 / 4.78 |
| M3b traces + dynamics | 0.035 | 8.3% | 135 | 4.99 / 4.06 / 4.77 |

- **Lexical overlap: memory arms were slightly lower than no memory**, the opposite sign to the 7B writer of AAM-E1-SIM: M2 - M0 -0.015 [-0.020, -0.011], M3b - M0 -0.014 [-0.019, -0.009], M3a - M0 -0.009 [-0.014, -0.004], M1 - M0 -0.004 [-0.010, 0.002]. Five of six streams agree on the sign for M2 and M3b.
- **Explicit references back were highest with traces** (M3a - M0 +0.078 [0.033, 0.122], M3b - M0 +0.072 [0.039, 0.111], M2 - M0 +0.039 [0.006, 0.078]), the same ordering as in AAM-E1-SIM. The rate of M0 is not zero (1.1%), so a model that was given no notes still produces some phrases the pattern accepts.
- **Judge `deepseek-v4-pro` (the same provider as the writer, so open to self-preference, equally for every arm).** Informative on freshness (all copies noticed) and **uninformative on fit** (58% of the off-theme replacements noticed against the 75% required; fit sits at 5.0 for nearly everything). Quality: M2 +0.15 [0.09, 0.21] and M1 +0.11 [0.03, 0.18] above M0, traces equal to M0 (+0.01, -0.01), traces 0.14 to 0.16 below raw retrieval. Freshness: M1 +0.09 [0.03, 0.15], M2 +0.07 [0.01, 0.13] above M0. The quality guard holds for every arm. Everything sits near the ceiling of the scale, so these are small differences on a compressed range.
- **The local `qwen2.5:14b-instruct` judge** (the cross-family judge here) ran on the same entries; its results are added to `judge-qwen14b-report.md` when the run ends, and are used only if it passes its controls, which it did not in AAM-E1-SIM.

**Replication verdict, by the rule stated in advance: not replicated.** E1 gave no named reading and E1-DS gives "nothing moves"; the sign of the lexical effect differs by writer (more overlap with notes for the 7B model, slightly less for the strong model), while the ordering of explicit references back (traces above raw retrieval above nothing) is the same in both. A strong writer given raw notes is rated slightly better by a same-family judge; the 7B writer given the same was rated worse by a cross-family one. The dependence on the writer is the result; no claim about memory in general follows.

## Erratum: the judge model (written on 2026-11-01 after the owner's objection)

The same-family judge named in this preregistration and used in the Results above was `deepseek-v4-pro`. **The owner had authorised only the flash model**, and the pro model was used without asking; that was a mistake. It is now blocked at the proxy (`scripts/flash_only_proxy.py`), the judge was **run again with `deepseek-flash`** (`judgments-flash.jsonl`, `judge-flash-report.md`, `judge-flash-results.json`) and the pro files are kept, labelled, as a secondary check already paid for. Deterministic results are unaffected.

`deepseek-flash` judging notes written by `deepseek-flash` is the same model on both sides, so a self-preference is possible, equally for every arm. It passed the planted controls on both dimensions (copies noticed 100%, off-theme replacements 92%), so unlike pro it is informative on fit as well. Results (fit / freshness / quality): M0 4.98 / 3.48 / 4.01, M1 5.00 / 3.88 / 4.19, M2 4.99 / 3.72 / 4.13, M3a 4.98 / 3.48 / 4.03, M3b 4.97 / 3.52 / 4.04. Against M0: M1 freshness +0.41 [0.31, 0.51] and quality +0.18 [0.09, 0.26], M2 freshness +0.24 [0.13, 0.34] and quality +0.12 [0.04, 0.21], traces no different (quality +0.02 and +0.03); M3a - M2: freshness -0.24 [-0.35, -0.13], quality -0.11 [-0.19, -0.02]. The preregistered cross-family judge, the local `qwen2.5:14b-instruct` (`judge-qwen14b-report.md`), **failed its planted controls again** (67% of the copies noticed on freshness and 0% of the off-theme replacements on fit, against 75% required) and scored almost everything 5, so it is not used. The picture is the one reported above: a strong writer is not hurt by raw notes and is judged slightly better, traces are neutral, and the 7B result does not replicate. The quality guard holds for every arm.
