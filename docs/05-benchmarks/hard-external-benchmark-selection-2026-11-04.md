Checked on 4 November 2026 following the owner's request to replace easy closed-slot demonstrations with difficult external tests. These suites stress different failure modes; no single length or vendor headline defines the hardest benchmark. Passing software tests, retrieving a labelled session and answering a question correctly are distinct outcomes.

## Why execute FactConsolidation first

It directly attacks the prior pilot's supplied routing, easy evidence selection and single-slot answer contract. A million-character counterfactual archive plus multi-hop questions can expose retrieval omissions and stale/world-knowledge answers. The official-style exact endpoint makes a positive or negative answer auditable without adding a permissive model judge. All 100 questions of the hard variant are used; results cannot be improved by selecting only easy questions or counting a successful context lookup as a correct answer.

The [frozen plan](../../data/lab/hard-memory-external-v1/PLAN.md) declares source versions, raw archive storage, ordinary kernel versus unlocked BM25 diagnostic, output budgets, local/Flash call limits, gold separation and readiness target before inference. The [previous LongMemEval bridge](../../data/lab/longmemeval-bridge-v0/execution-v0.1/report.md) already measured session retrieval on 36 public cases; repeating its high Recall@5 would not answer the owner's request for a hard end-to-end test. The previous pack-reader fixture was also already spent and saturated; it is preserved rather than relabelled as new hard evidence.

## Reporting rules

- Publish the exact variant, denominator, context budget, prompt/reader changes and score type. A bounded adapter result is not an official full-agent leaderboard entry.
- Keep failures, UNKNOWN, malformed outputs and unsupported answers. Do not repair raw answers using scoring gold or change thresholds after viewing this run.
- Preserve the original eight-slot pilot as a narrow engineering result. It establishes neither superiority over memory companies nor recall over two centuries.
- Local models are preferred where adequate. Only official DeepSeek V4.1 Flash may be called through a paid API; never execute downloaded benchmark defaults that would silently choose another model/provider.
- Full source data, prompts and completions stay in the local external evidence directory; public records contain pinned identities, content-free case references, metrics, usage and decisions.

These are primary-source checks, not a survey of vendor leaderboard claims. The source snapshots and datasets are cached locally with their observed revisions; only the suite explicitly marked executed in its result record may be described as tested.
