# Future-utility causal and privacy primary-source audit

Status: adversarial design audit; no natural capture or causal claim authorized

Audited: 2026-08-23

Applies to: `PMLAB-UTILITY-001`, telemetry schema v0.1, and the proposed T1-T4 ladder

## Bottom line

The T0.1 instrument is a useful synthetic integrity construction, but it is not yet an admissible measurement system for natural project history. Four distinctions now become mandatory:

1. a memory, a context bundle, a task, and a dependence cluster are different experimental units;
2. an observation deadline makes censoring visible but does not make it ignorable;
3. a logged number called `propensity` is useful only when it is the probability of the realized action under the exact logging policy and has adequate support;
4. a content hash is a linkable pseudonym and integrity aid, not anonymous data or an erasure mechanism.

The next valid step is not T1 capture. It is a frozen audit of the T0.1 contract, followed by synthetic lifecycle and causal-design fixtures. T1 remains locked until the audit findings and the governance, outcome, missingness, and erasure contracts are closed.

## Evidence-to-design audit

| Project assumption | Primary-source result | Audit conclusion | Required consequence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A dependence-cluster ID solves shared-memory dependence | Hudgens and Halloran define distinct direct, indirect, total, and overall effects when one unit's assignment can affect another unit's outcome. Aronow and Samii require an assignment design, an exposure mapping, and an estimand under general interference. | Necessary metadata, insufficient identification | Predeclare the unit, interference boundary, exposure mapping, assignment mechanism, and bundle-level estimand. Clustered standard errors alone do not repair a wrong estimand. |
| One exposed memory can receive the task outcome | Interference work shows outcomes can depend on joint assignments, not only the unit's own treatment. | Rejected for multi-memory contexts | Make the primary estimand the effect of an assigned context bundle. Individual-memory credit requires a factorial, leave-one-out, or other identified contrast and must not be copied to every exposed item. |
| A fixed observation window solves delayed outcomes | Hernan and Robins show that loss to follow-up can induce selection bias; inverse-probability censoring weights require exchangeability, positivity, consistency, and a correctly handled time structure. | Deadline discipline is useful but censoring may remain informative | Log eligibility for observation, censoring time and reason, predictors available before censoring, and complete outcome denominators. Freeze a complete-case, bounds, sensitivity, or IPCW plan before outcomes are opened. |
| Recording `propensity` enables off-policy evaluation | Off-policy estimators evaluate a target policy from data generated by another policy. Importance-based and doubly robust estimators depend on the logging-policy probabilities and can have severe variance or model-misspecification problems. | Field presence is insufficient | Record the probability of the realized action, action space, exact policy version/state, random seed or assignment receipt, and safety overrides. Report overlap, zero-support actions, weight distribution, effective sample size, clipping, and estimator sensitivity. |
| Doubly robust means robust to arbitrary logging defects | Doubly robust estimators combine outcome modeling and importance weighting; the guarantee is conditional, not immunity to both models being wrong or support being absent. | Rejected shortcut | Never use `doubly robust` as a quality label. Verify support and run direct, IPS, self-normalized/clipped, and DR sensitivity analyses against a randomized or simulated ground truth before T4. |
| SHA-256 identifiers are anonymous and safe to release | ENISA identifies direct hashing as weak pseudonymisation because brute force, dictionary search, and guesswork can recover low-entropy identifiers; keyed MACs or random mappings change the threat model. | Rejected | Classify hashes as linkable pseudonymous data when derived from user content. Use purpose-separated random opaque IDs for joins; consider keyed HMAC only after key governance. Never publish content hashes of low-entropy private strings. |
| Append-only telemetry and user erasure are the same design problem | GDPR requires minimisation, storage limitation, protection by design/default, appropriate security, and erasure in applicable circumstances. Append-only transparency logs prove consistency, not deletion. | Direct tension, not yet solved | Separate minimal audit receipts from content/linkage storage. Define export, rectification, tombstone, erasure propagation, index rebuild, backup, processor, and verification semantics. A tombstone or crypto-shredding proposal is not by itself a legal-compliance claim. |
| Hashing JSON without a byte contract gives stable integrity | RFC 8785 exists because hashing/signing JSON requires an invariant representation. | Current hash semantics are underspecified | For every digest, record whether it covers exact source bytes or a named canonicalization profile. Do not silently reserialize Unicode, numbers, key order, or whitespace before verification. |
| Local-first means private by default | GDPR and NIST both treat access, lifecycle, processors, security, reassessment, and risk as explicit controls. | Rejected | Create a threat model and Current/Target privacy profile. Decide authentication, authorization, encryption at rest, key custody, access audit, export, backup, and external-worker boundaries before natural capture. |

## Causal contract required before T3

Every randomized exposure experiment must freeze this tuple:

```text
unit
population and eligibility
assignment mechanism
action space and realized-action propensity
exposure mapping, including competing memories
interference boundary
treatment versions
primary bundle-level estimand
outcome and observation window
censoring and missingness strategy
safety exclusions and rescue rule
estimator, variance method, diagnostics, and rejection rule
```

### Primary unit and estimand

The safest default is a task-level assignment of an entire context bundle. A task outcome may then support an intention-to-treat contrast between bundle policies. It does not identify the value of each memory in the bundle.

An individual-memory estimand becomes admissible only when the design varies that memory while controlling or randomizing relevant competing memories, or when a defensible exposure mapping reduces the joint assignment to named exposure conditions. The mapping is a scientific assumption and must receive sensitivity tests.

### Interference

`dependence_cluster_id` is retained, but its meaning must be frozen: shared canonical memory, shared user/session, repeated task family, or another mechanism. Different mechanisms may require different clusters. Partial interference may be assumed only when the project can defend that tasks in separate clusters cannot affect each other through memory updates, user learning, policy drift, or shared summaries.

### Propensity and support

The current scalar field can validate syntax but not policy provenance. Before T3, an assignment receipt must bind the candidate set, action space, exact logging-policy version and state, realized arm, realized-arm probability, safety overrides, and deterministic inputs. An arm with zero probability for a stratum cannot be evaluated off-policy for that stratum.

Required diagnostics include minimum propensity, overlap by stratum, weight quantiles and maximum, effective sample size, fraction clipped, policy-version counts, and results under multiple registered estimators. Low effective sample size or policy drift is a redesign signal, not a reason to report a favorable estimator.

### Censoring and missingness

The observation-window event must preserve three separate facts:

- the outcome was structurally inapplicable;
- the outcome existed but was unobserved or lost;
- the outcome had not occurred by the deadline.

Complete-case analysis is not automatically valid. IPCW is allowed only with its identification assumptions, predictors, model diagnostics, positivity check, and sensitivity analysis frozen. Otherwise report bounds or descriptive missingness and withhold a causal claim.

## Privacy and lifecycle contract required before T1

Natural capture requires a data inventory that follows every allowed datum through:

```text
source -> canonical record -> telemetry -> indexes -> derived tables
       -> review packets -> external processors -> backups -> export/erasure verification
```

For each edge, record purpose, legal/user authorization basis as applicable, sensitivity, controller/processor role, location, access scope, retention, and deletion propagation. This repository provides engineering research, not legal advice or a declaration of GDPR compliance.

### Identifier rules

- Prefer random opaque event, task, memory, subject, and assignment IDs.
- Keep a reversible identity mapping only when the declared purpose requires it, in separately protected storage.
- Do not treat an unkeyed digest of names, email addresses, short messages, common facts, or enumerated content as anonymous.
- If keyed pseudonyms are justified, specify key generation, custody, rotation, compromise response, destruction, and domain separation.
- Specify digest purpose and representation: exact bytes or a named canonicalization scheme such as JCS where appropriate.

### Append-only versus erasure

The project should test a split design:

1. a minimal integrity stream containing opaque, non-content receipts and permitted aggregate facts;
2. separately encrypted content and linkage material with explicit retention and deletion control;
3. append-only correction/tombstone events that demonstrate the request and propagation result without retaining the erased content;
4. rebuildable indexes and derived datasets with deletion verification;
5. documented backup and external-processor propagation.

This is a candidate architecture to audit, not proof that cryptographic deletion, tombstones, or immutable logs satisfy every applicable right or exception.

## Independent-audit boundary

The project can prepare and mechanically validate a blind packet, but it cannot make its own review institutionally independent. An author-operated external model can provide a cross-family adversarial review and may discover defects. It cannot serve as legal advice, a DPIA, a human privacy approval, or an independent statistical reproduction.

The audit packet therefore asks fixed falsification questions, hashes its inputs, requires conflict/prior-exposure disclosure, and produces only one of these T1 recommendations:

- `deny` — unresolved blocking defect;
- `conditional` — exact repairs and re-audit required;
- `allow_shadow_only` — natural, no-model, no-ranking-change capture may begin under the reviewed scope.

No reviewer in this packet can authorize randomized withholding, adaptive ranking, retention, deletion, consolidation, or emotional salience.

## Locked decisions

- Keep T1-T4, adaptive policy, and canonical mutation disabled.
- Add no natural user data to the telemetry fixtures.
- Treat bundle-level ITT as the default causal target.
- Treat hashes of user-derived content as potentially personal/linkable.
- Build the audit packet and lifecycle fixtures before adding the local sink.
- Preserve negative reviews and unresolved findings in the public research history.
