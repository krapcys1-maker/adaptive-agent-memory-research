## Research question, system, and method

The study asked whether extinction training shortly after reactivation persistently modifies a conditioned fear memory rather than creating a competing extinction memory. In Experiment 1, included participants received extinction 10 minutes after one reminder (`n=20`), six hours after a reminder (`n=23`), or without a reminder (`n=22`). Skin-conductance response (SCR) to partially reinforced colored-square/shock conditioning was tested after 24 hours. Nineteen included participants returned 10–14 months later. Experiment 2 used a within-subject comparison of a reminded and non-reminded conditioned cue (`n=18`).

## Extracted results

- At the 24-hour test, group × time was significant (`F(2,62)=4.63`, `p<0.05`). Recovery from the last extinction trial to first re-extinction trial appeared in the no-reminder (`t=2.69`, `p<0.05`) and six-hour (`t=2.66`, `p<0.05`) groups but not the 10-minute group (`t=0.28`, nonsignificant; Figure 1).
- At 10–14 months, 19/65 original participants entered the final analysis (10-minute `n=8`; collapsed six-hour/no-reminder `n=11`). The group × stage interaction was only marginal (`F(1,17)=2.78`, one-tailed `p<0.07`); one-tailed follow-ups reported reinstatement in the collapsed comparison but not 10-minute group (Figure 2).
- In Experiment 2, reinstatement occurred for the non-reminded CSb+ (`t=2.16`, `p<0.05`) but not reminded CSa+ (`t=0.22`, nonsignificant), with a stimulus × time interaction (`F(2,51)=5.14`, `p<0.01`; Figure 3).

## Limitations and challenge pass

- The paper itself states that the behavioral study does not directly demonstrate reconsolidation as the mediating process; timing and the chunking/gap between cue presentations are confounded.
- Analyses included only participants meeting acquisition/extinction criteria. A later registered replication reported that the published exclusion description did not match the original application, creating a major reproducibility concern.
- The one-year result used 19 participants, collapsed two comparison groups, relied on one-tailed follow-ups, and had only a marginal interaction.
- SCR indexes conditioned autonomic responding, not the complete memory. Absence of expressed fear does not uniquely imply deletion or rewriting of the underlying association.
- Experiment 2 is small and uses multiple within-subject comparisons.

## Project relevance and falsifiable hypothesis

A correction may alter which representation controls action without erasing the original record. Compare destructive overwrite with append-only supersession and coexisting context-scoped beliefs. Probe spontaneous return, old-context renewal, and reinstatement by old cues after delays. Reject destructive updating if old evidence is needed for audit, rollback, or detecting why a prior action was rational at its valid time.
