## Research question, system, and method

The preregistered study directly replicated the critical reactivation-plus-10-minute-extinction and no-reactivation groups of Schiller et al. Experiment 1. It recruited 246 participants and replaced prespecified exclusions until 124 remained (`n=62/group`; 105 female). The sample provided reported 90% power for a meta-analytic effect of `g=0.53`. Acquisition, extinction, spontaneous recovery, and a same-session supplementary reinstatement test used differential SCR.

## Extracted results

- Both groups acquired and extinguished differential conditioned responding.
- Both showed spontaneous recovery: reactivation `t(61)=4.96`, `p<0.001`, `d=0.63`; no-reactivation `t(61)=4.46`, `p<0.001`, `d=0.57`.
- Recovery did not differ between groups (`t(122)=0.93`, `p=0.35`, `d=0.17`), and was numerically larger after reactivation.
- Both groups showed reinstatement; the between-group reinstatement difference was effectively zero (`p=0.93`, `d=0.02`).
- The authors found no advantage under alternative/raw-score analyses either.

## Limitations and challenge pass

- The final sample depended on extensive acquisition/extinction exclusions: 82 failed acquisition criteria, 15 failed extinction criteria, and 23 were excluded for other reasons. This narrows the population and exposes sensitivity to selection rules.
- The replication omitted the original six-hour group and one-year follow-up; its reinstatement test was intentionally not an exact replication. The primary spontaneous-recovery comparison was direct.
- The study reproduced the reported protocol as clarified with original authors, but the need to replace initially reported exclusion rules is itself a reproducibility failure in the target study.
- A null result for this behavioral procedure does not refute molecular reconsolidation across species, memory systems, pharmacological interventions, or genuinely different boundary conditions.
- SCR is one response system and cannot by itself distinguish storage change from access, inhibition, or response selection.

## Project relevance and falsifiable hypothesis

Memory-update behavior must be robust to reasonable inclusion, timing, and scoring choices. Pre-register correction rules and report all records, not only cases that show clean acquisition/update. A revision mechanism fails if its apparent benefit depends on post-hoc eligibility filters or if stale beliefs return under delayed, old-context, or reinstatement probes.
