## Research question, system, and method

Rats learned an eight-day W-track alternation task with a memory-demanding outbound rule and a simpler inbound return rule. Online detection triggered ventral hippocampal commissure stimulation that terminated awake CA1 SWRs within about 25 ms and inhibited spiking for roughly 100 ms. Groups were SWR disruption (`n=6`), delayed control stimulation 150–200 ms after detection (`n=4`), and unimplanted/no stimulation (`n=4`). Interleaved rest sessions measured post-experience SWR reactivation.

## Extracted results

- SWR-disrupted rats performed worse on outbound trials across eight days (group `p<0.001`; group × day `p<0.01`) and reached learning criteria later (`p<0.01`; Figure 2A–D).
- All six disrupted animals ranked below all eight controls on learning/final outbound performance; the reported chance probability of this separation was `<0.0007`.
- Inbound performance was unaffected (group `p>0.33`; group × day `p>0.5`; learning criteria `p>0.5`; final performance `p>0.48`; Figure 2E–H).
- CA1 place-field stability, peak rates, and sizes did not differ between disruption and delayed-control groups (`p>0.5` for the main reported comparisons; Figure 3).
- Post-experience rest SWR rate and reactivation relationships were intact in both groups, separating the awake manipulation from later rest reactivation (Figure 4).
- Three delayed-control rats switched to disruption on days 9–10 showed a performance decline in supplementary analyses.

## Limitations and challenge pass

- Small group sizes make the delayed control particularly thin, even though the complete rank separation is striking.
- Electrical stimulation interrupted the SWR-associated population event, not only decoded replay content. The causal result is for awake SWRs/associated activity, while forward/reverse replay as the specific operative content remains a hypothesis.
- The selective outbound deficit argues against global damage but also narrows the claim: simpler inbound learning and post-experience reactivation remained intact.
- The task combines retrieval of recent locations, rule learning, planning, reward linkage, and working memory; it does not isolate one mnemonic computation.
- Group nesting and multiple cell/pair measurements must not be treated as larger independent animal samples.

## Project relevance and falsifiable hypothesis

Near-decision maintenance may help most when a decision must bind current state to recent and remote evidence, rather than for a simple cached return rule. Test replay/retrieval triggers across tasks with and without multi-step history dependence. Reject always-on online replay if it does not show an interaction with memory demand, or if a direct evidence retrieval call provides the same benefit at lower cost.
