## Research question, system, and method

Adult male Lister Hooded rats learned one-trial food locations in an event arena over repeated within-subject conditions, paired with hippocampal-slice weak/strong LTP protocols. The study tested whether unrelated novelty near encoding rescues weak 24-hour spatial memory through hippocampal D1/D5 signaling and protein synthesis.

## Extracted results

- Weak one-pellet encoding supported 30-minute but not 24-hour memory; stronger three-pellet encoding supported 24-hour memory (Conditions 1–2; Figures 1–2, pp. 1–2).
- Five-minute novel-box exploration 30 minutes after weak encoding rescued 24-hour memory (`t(10)=4.05`, `p<0.01`; Figure 3E, pp. 3–4).
- Hippocampal D1/D5 antagonist during novelty blocked rescue (`F(1,10)=9.95`, `p<0.01`). Immediate post-novelty anisomycin blocked it, while a six-hour-delayed infusion did not (Figure 3E–F, pp. 3–4).
- Novelty one hour before strong encoding rescued memory despite high-dose D1/D5 blockade during encoding (Figure 4E: vehicle `t(8)=5.27`, `p<0.001`; blockade `t(8)=4.94`, `p<0.01`; pp. 4–5).
- A within-animal good/poor/rescued meta-analysis produced the preregistered qualitative V shape in 14 of 16 rats (`chi²=9`, `p<0.005`; Figure 5, p. 5).

## Limitations and challenge pass

- One male rat strain, appetitive spatial task, small condition-specific samples, many sequential conditions over six months, and pharmacological specificity limit generality.
- Novelty was unrelated to the remembered location yet occurred within a timing window; this is not evidence that all novel items themselves deserve durable storage.
- The authors discuss alternative novelty-dopamine accounts and acknowledge that STC does not uniquely predict every observation (Discussion, pp. 5–6).
- LTP analogy is mechanistically informative but is not identical to behavioral memory.

## Project relevance and falsifiable hypothesis

Separate a short-lived eligibility tag from the later modulatory event that promotes persistence. Test whether provisional records promoted after a verified outcome/novelty signal within a bounded window outperform immediate permanent writes. Reject if delayed promotion misses critical events or indiscriminately preserves unrelated novelty/noise.
