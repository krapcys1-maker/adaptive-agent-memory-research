## Corrections to the abstract-level synthesis

1. BREAK does **not** supply a compositional or semantic-template split. Its split follows the underlying datasets and avoids shared contexts; our grouped split remains a new benchmark control.
2. QDMR does **not** perform schema/entity grounding. Obligation discovery and scope linking therefore remain separate evaluated stages.
3. Exact representation match is a strict diagnostic, not semantic correctness. BREAK and the schema-linking audit both expose substantial exact-match false negatives.
4. Spider-Syn is useful evidence for lexical fragility, but its ManualMAS result is partly an oracle-style upper bound because it uses the benchmark substitutions.
5. Temporal graph correctness and temporal denotation correctness can diverge. The mapper must report both, plus unsupported/unresolved behavior.

## Metric consequences frozen from the audit

Every mapping arm must expose three non-substitutable views:

- **strict form**: exact normalized obligation set, typed fields, and canonical serialization;
- **structure**: operator nodes, arguments, dependencies, links, and graph validity;
- **denotation/action**: execution-equivalent answer obligations, normalized time intervals, certificate queries, and downstream N0-N3 decisions.

No average may hide a critical omission, silent ambiguity resolution, or false N2/N3 closure. Per-stage oracle ceilings are required so failures can be localized rather than relabeled as end-to-end “memory loss.”
