# HUM-019 — Direct replications challenge unconditional human reconsolidation-updating

- Status: `challenged`

## Scope and operational definition

- Population/species/system: healthy human participants in conditioned-fear and procedural/declarative sequence tasks.
- Intervention or comparison: reminder plus extinction/new learning versus no reminder or original expected decrement.
- Measured outcome: SCR recovery/reinstatement and sequence performance after 24 hours.
- Timescale: three-day protocols.

## Contradictions, null results, and boundary conditions

The original Schiller fear study and other conceptual replications reported positive effects. These nulls do not test every pharmacological intervention, species, prediction-error condition, memory strength, or memory system.

## Alternative explanations

Original and replication differences may reflect selection, measurement, timing, memory strength, prediction error, or chance. Unspecified boundary conditions cannot rescue an engineering rule without prospective tests.

## Computational translation

- Abstract problem: avoid corrupting durable state during ordinary access.
- Candidate mechanism: non-mutating reads plus explicit verified revision transactions.
- Simpler baseline: automatic read-modify-write.
- Predicted benefit: fewer false updates and recoverable history.
- Predicted failure: update latency or routing complexity causes stale action.
- Rejection criterion: explicit revision cannot meet urgent-correction targets or offers no safety advantage.
