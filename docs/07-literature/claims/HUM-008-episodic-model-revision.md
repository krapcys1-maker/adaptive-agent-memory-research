## Contradictions, null results, and boundary conditions

The Perspective states that variable-rate compression's ability to prevent structure-learning failures remains to be tested. Surprise may indicate noise or attack rather than a better model. Emotional salience is discussed as a future extension, not validated as the mechanism.

## Alternative explanations

Benefits may arise from greater storage or replay compute rather than episodic fidelity. A better ensemble/uncertainty model could avoid the structural error without storing raw anomalies.

## Computational translation

- Abstract problem: current compression model destroys evidence needed to revise itself.
- Candidate mechanism: high-fidelity exception buffer with model-change, trust, and delayed-utility gates.
- Simpler baseline: uniform reservoir or recency buffer.
- Predicted benefit: better delayed rule-change and rare-exception recovery.
- Predicted failure: excessive noise/poison retention.
- Rejection criterion: no delayed structure-recovery benefit over simpler buffers, or safety/storage cost erases it.
