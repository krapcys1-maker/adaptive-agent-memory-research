## Contradictions, null results, and boundary conditions

Cortisol effects may be U-shaped and/or require norepinephrine. Different tasks and endogenous versus exogenous manipulations yield opposing control accounts. The review predates later replication/meta-analysis.

## Alternative explanations

Stress may impair discovery/awareness of the spatial strategy or executive control rather than directly strengthen habit memory. Preserved performance can hide strategy change.
