# Comparative memory source seeds

Status: in-progress

These are starting points for citation snowballing. Each row reports what the study directly supports and what it does not establish.

| Track | Primary study | Direct observation | Important boundary | Candidate computational abstraction |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Bird episodic-like memory | [Clayton & Dickinson 1998](https://doi.org/10.1038/26216) | scrub jays used what, where, and when information about cached foods with different perishability | behavioral criteria do not establish subjective recollection | jointly bound event fields and type-conditioned validity horizons |
| Cephalopod episodic-like memory | [Jozet-Alves et al. 2013](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cub.2013.10.021) | cuttlefish adjusted foraging using what, where, and elapsed time | one task family and no claim of a human episodic experience | convergent task solution and multidimensional episode keys |
| Neural habituation | [Carew & Kandel 1973](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.182.4117.1158) | spaced training produced long-lasting habituation with correlated synaptic decrement in Aplysia | simple reflex learning, not general memory architecture | frequency-dependent suppression, spacing, and substrate-level validation |
| Human motor consolidation | [Brashers-Krug et al. 1996](https://doi.org/10.1038/382252a0) | a second motor task disrupted the first immediately but not after a delay | task-specific psychophysics and an older small experimental literature | procedural interference tests and delayed stabilization |
| Skeletal-muscle history | [Seaborne et al. 2018](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-018-20287-3) | prior loading left methylation signatures and altered reloading response | eight men; molecular history is not neural skill memory | persistent priming and savings during reacquisition |
| Trained innate immunity | [Kaufmann et al. 2018](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2017.12.031) | BCG altered hematopoietic progenitors and generated macrophages with enhanced protection in mice | immune training is not episodic or semantic recall | long-lived upstream state that changes later responders |
| Cellular transcriptional memory | [Brickner et al. 2007](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pbio.0050081) | recently repressed yeast genes retained a nuclear/chromatin state and reactivated faster across generations | gene regulation under specific conditions | compact primed state, hysteresis, and faster reactivation |
| Non-neural habituation | [Boisseau et al. 2016](https://doi.org/10.1098/rspb.2016.0446) | Physarum reduced avoidance of a repeated harmless stimulus and later recovered the response | habituation versus adaptation and mechanism boundaries require scrutiny | suppress repeated harmless signals while retaining recovery conditions |
| Prokaryotic immune history | [Barrangou et al. 2007](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1138140) | acquired CRISPR spacers conferred sequence-specific phage resistance | sequence defense is not flexible cognitive memory | append-like signatures, order, bounded capacity, and exact threat matching |

## First snowballing questions

- Which findings replicated across laboratories, species, tasks, sexes, and timescales?
- Which effects depend on stress, reward, ecological relevance, or training schedule?
- What are the null results and failed replications?
- Which terms changed meaning across subfields?
- Which apparent memories are selection across generations rather than learning within an individual lifetime?
- Which mechanisms preserve original evidence, and which only preserve a changed response tendency?
