## Why this rebuild differs from v2's

Every mechanism tested to date (D through I) operates read-side or query-side on content Mem0's own write-time extraction call already chose to keep. Nothing has touched the extraction call itself except one narrow, already-`CALIBRATION_FAILED` instruction (`EVENT_TIME_RESOLUTION_AT_EXTRACTION`, Ledger Entry E). Discovery agents were explicitly directed to prioritize write-time/ingest-time mechanisms, and to verify any code claim against `external/venvs/mem0/Lib/site-packages` specifically — not `external/repos/mem0ai__mem0`, the unpinned upstream clone that produced v2's false `CODE_VERIFIED` claim.

3. **`EXTRACTION_COMPLETENESS_VERIFICATION_PASS`** (composite 567, **`STRONG_CANDIDATE`**) — one additional write-time LLM call after normal extraction: re-read the raw session against the facts already extracted, and append anything missed (especially individual items within lists). Structurally orthogonal to `RAW_PROVENANCE` (which patches query/answer time, not ingest time).
