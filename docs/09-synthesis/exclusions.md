# Exclusions and Rejected Shortcuts

Status: in-progress

| Idea | Current status | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| “Store everything in one vector database” | rejected as complete architecture | does not solve temporal validity, provenance, procedures, contradiction, or utility |
| “Context window equals human short-term memory” | rejected as literal claim | useful partial analogy but important functional differences |
| “Every unretrieved event is useless” | rejected | absence of a future query is censored evidence, not a negative utility label |
| “Retrieval count equals usefulness” | rejected | confounded by ranking exposure and does not establish causal benefit |
| “Emotion score means the agent feels emotion” | rejected | operational signals do not imply subjective experience |
| “A higher end-to-end QA score proves better memory” | rejected | may reflect reader model, prompt, judge, contamination, or reasoning rather than memory |
| “Consolidation may overwrite source episodes” | rejected for early phases | irreversible detail loss and summary drift are unacceptable research risks |
| “Start with a large neural network” | deferred | simple rules and classical models are required baselines |
