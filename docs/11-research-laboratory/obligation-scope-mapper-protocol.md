# Obligation decomposer and scope-mapper protocol

Status: construction and post-freeze challenge completed; both deployable arms rejected; independent review absent

## Purpose

`PMLAB-MAP-001` isolates the only critical failure left by the PMLAB-CLOSURE v1 construction arm: a question may contain multiple obligations whose predicates and closure states differ. The experiment must evaluate decomposition separately from entity, predicate/schema, temporal, namespace, and certificate mapping.

## Freeze order

1. Freeze the intermediate schema, operator inventory, gold obligation policy, and asymmetric error utilities.
2. Freeze semantic template groups before generating language/paraphrase variants.
3. Freeze construction and unseen-composition splits by template group and schema, never by row.
4. Freeze gold annotations and independent review dispositions.
5. Commit corpus/manifest before implementing deterministic or model-based arms.
6. Freeze each linker version before evaluating the full pipeline.
7. Reuse the PMLAB-CLOSURE v1 evaluator unchanged for downstream safety metrics.

## Minimum case artifacts

- raw query, language, reference time, timezone, and user/schema context;
- gold obligations with operators and dependencies;
- entity catalog with aliases, types, collisions, and NIL cases;
- predicate/namespace schema and versioned glossary;
- temporal normalization gold with ambiguity and unsupported labels;
- gold per-obligation certificate query shapes;
- template group, composition atoms, schema split, paraphrase group, and criticality;
- model-visible payload generated by stripping all gold and split metadata.

## Leakage controls

- opaque case IDs and factor names absent from prompts;
- one semantic/paraphrase/bilingual group in only one split;
- new composition split holds out compounds, not their atomic vocabulary;
- unseen-schema split holds out namespaces and surface labels;
- glossary candidates sourced before challenge text is authored;
- prompt examples cannot share entity, predicate phrase, time surface, or dependency template with challenge cases;
- learned/LLM arms receive the same versioned schema and reference clock.

## Promotion boundary

A construction pass validates serialization and scoring only. No mapper advances until it passes an unseen template-and-schema challenge, zero critical false-closure gate, bilingual audit, and independent label review. A cloud-model arm may be useful as a replaceable comparator, never as gold.

## Current construction artifact

`docs/11-research-laboratory/obligation-ir-schema-v0.md` defines the operator, scope, atomicity, split, utility, and metric contract. `data/lab/pmlab-obligation-mapping-dev-v0/` contains 28 semantic groups expanded to 56 paired PL/EN cases. It covers all 13 operators and includes ambiguity, NIL, authorization, temporal, closure, unsupported-structure, and structure-versus-denotation metric fixtures. It remains inspectable development data and may not be reported as held out.

## Post-freeze challenge result

The challenge corpus froze at `adc540f` after the deterministic runner and optional-model prompt were fixed. Its 14 semantic groups yield 28 paired PL/EN cases with entirely disjoint predicate, namespace, and catalog entity identifiers plus unseen complete composition signatures. Labels come from the same research process and are not independently reviewed.

The unchanged QDMR rules fell from construction obligation F1 0.922 to 0.409, with 18 critical omissions, ten false closures, and zero end-to-end exact cases. The unchanged `deepseek-v4-flash` prompt fell from 0.710 to 0.325, returned only 13/28 schema-valid predictions, made 17 critical omissions and two false closures, and reached 0.107 end-to-end exact. Both arms fail promotion. No post-challenge repair may be evaluated on this challenge as if it were still unseen.
