# Delayed-relevance recovery at a fixed budget: first experiment, proposal v0

**Status: proposal v0, with a first run recorded below.** On 2026-11-01 the lead built and ran it once as an exploratory study (`data/lab/aam-e0-delayed-relevance/`, informal id AAM-E0); see the status section that follows. It is not registered, the owner has not approved the design, and no money was spent. Registering it needs the owner's decision (section 6). It answers item 6 of the [research vision](../00-project/research-vision-2026-11.md) ("recommend the smallest useful first experiment") and uses the provisional ids V-H1 and V-H5 defined in the [direction review](../00-project/research-direction-review-2026-11.md), because plain "H1" already names corpus H1 here.

## Status after the first run (2026-11-01)

The design below was built as AAM-E0 and run once on the frozen test seeds. The as-built design and the numbers are in `data/lab/aam-e0-delayed-relevance/README.md`; this section lists what differs from the proposal and what the run showed.

**Result.** Stage 1 (20 worlds, 240 probes, run once): NOT SUPPORTED. In the primary cell (C3, context 250 words, retained memory 10%, strict gold) arm D reaches 0.075 and the best comparator, Sum-raw, 0.125; the difference is -0.050 with a 95% interval of [-0.125, +0.0125]. Dense retrieval over everything (F2) reaches 0.200, and D is not distinguishable from shuffled pointers (S 0.049). A caveat found afterwards: at R = 10% D stores the gold event in 20% of the probes, so no association rule could have exceeded 0.20, and the largest possible gap to the best comparator was 0.075, below the 0.15 threshold; the primary cell could not have confirmed the hypothesis. At R = 25% D stores the gold in 50% of the probes and puts it in the context in 8.75% (an oracle on D's own store reaches 50%), so the loss is in association, not in storage. The result concerns this generator and surface-concept traces; it says nothing about traces written by a model.

**What was built differently from this proposal**
- Budgets are in words and scaled to a 150-event archive of about 2,470 words (events of 12 to 35 words; the first draft of this section said 3,500, an unchecked estimate): C = 100, 250 and 500 words, R = 10% and 25%. The 1,000- and 4,000-token budgets of section 3 would hold most of such an archive.
- The cells are C1 (about 40% lexical overlap), C2 (no shared content word, same topic) and C3 (no shared content word, relevance by role); C3 is the primary cell. The corpus is a new generator, `scripts/corpus/goalshift.py`, not an addition to `FAMILIES`.
- Gold-in-context is strict: the reference code must be in the delivered text. Crediting a summary by event id (section 3) made the development-chosen best comparator a spurious draw, so that measure is reported as secondary.
- Write-time importance is uninformative by construction. The first version drew it from the event kind and so told every forgetting arm which events mattered; independent review found this, and it was fixed before the test seeds were generated.
- Arms: A, F1, F2, B3, Del, Sum, Sum-raw, D, O (oracle with cheaper traces), Oc (oracle on D's own store and costs) and S (the mean of 20 pointer shuffles). Stage 1b (signal validity) was not built. Stage 2 was written and not run.
- The test seeds are fixed by digests in `config.json`, not by `scripts/sealed_split.py`, so the study is unsealed and exploratory. Under the doctrine's selection table a study that builds a fresh corpus is Tier C and needs an I1 sealed split; this one carries no tier claim.
- Independent review covered the first version only (three agents; the defects were fixed before the test seeds were generated). The final version has had none.

**What the independent reviews of this document asked for, and where it stands.** The arms B and B2 named in sections 3, 5 and 7 do not exist; read F2 (whole-archive retrieval). The overlap measure was never defined; E0 measures it in the arms' own token space (one stop-word list and a crude stemmer). The decision rules are now a complete function: SUPPORTED only if D minus the best of Del, Sum and Sum-raw is at least 0.15 and the interval's lower bound is above zero, otherwise NOT SUPPORTED, plus two qualifiers that can fire together (Sum-raw within 0.05 of D; F2 at or above D); thresholds are inclusive with a 1e-9 tolerance. Precision: the observed 95% interval for the primary contrast was 0.14 wide (about +/-0.07 at these low rates), so a true gap of 0.15 would have been resolved; the limiting factor was the ceiling, not the sample, and more worlds would narrow the interval roughly with the square root of their number at no cost in Stage 1. A feasibility gate is missing from Stage 0 and is added to section 4.

## 1. The question

At equal budgets, does a compact **associative trace** kept in the active store, pointing back to a preserved raw archive, recover an old, low-priority experience that only becomes relevant after the agent's goal changes, better than the alternatives that forget the same experience (deleting it, or replacing it with a summary)? The claim is about a *regime* (delayed relevance after a goal change, small budgets, little shared wording), not about a new mechanism. A null result, "a summary index with expansion to the raw event is as good", or "plain retrieval over everything is as good", is a complete answer; whether and where to publish it is the owner's decision.

## 3. Design

**Corpus (new, deterministic, no model).** A new family `GOALSHIFT` added to the history-family generator (a pure addition to `FAMILIES`; existing instances are not renumbered). One scenario holds about 150 experiences over 30 simulated days and 3 goal phases. In each phase a few early, low-salience experiences are *delayed-relevance targets*: they become relevant only after a later goal change, and the cue names the new goal in different words. Per probe the generator records the **content-word overlap** between cue and gold event and sets it at three levels (M0 about 40% as in corpus H1, M1 about 15%, M2 about 0 after stop-word removal). Each cue also gets at least two **decoys**: events that share a concept with the cue but are irrelevant, so a false association is measurable. Scenarios are generated from seeds: development seeds 0-4, test seeds 100-119 (20 scenarios, 12 delayed probes each, 240 paired probes), the test seeds fixed in a digest-checked configuration before Stage 1 (not a sealed split in the sense of I1; see the status section).

**Two budgets.** The *context budget* C is what the model is given per task: 250, 1,000 and 4,000 tokens (4,000 is the vision's starting figure), counted with one pinned tokenizer; the corpus-H1 convention of counting words is kept as a second column. The *retained-memory budget* R is the size of the representation that stays searchable in the active store, as a share of the raw archive: 10% and 25%. Everything else is **dormant**: still on disk (operational forgetting, never deletion), but reachable only through a pointer from something that stayed searchable. Arms that differ only in what they keep under R are the fair comparison for V-H1; arms with no forgetting are references, not rivals.

**Arms.** All arms read the same stream, fill context with the shared `_fill` rule of `scripts/corpus/arms.py` (no future leakage), and are given the same R and C.

| Arm | What it keeps and does | Role | Stage |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | Nothing: the goal and the cue only. | floor | 1 |
| F1, F2 | Everything searchable, no forgetting: lexical BM25 (existing FTS5 baseline) and dense (local MiniLM, as in `dense-probe-v0`). | reference ceiling: what conventional retrieval does when nothing is forgotten | 1 |
| B3 | Recency window: the newest experiences that fit C. | the default context window | 1 |
| Del | Raw text of the K highest write-time-importance events (a static score from novelty, recency and goal relevance at ingest) plus recent ones, under R; the rest dormant and unreachable. | the honest "deletion" comparator: forgetting without a trace | 1 |
| Sum | Dormant events replaced by summary entries under R, with no pointer. Stage 1: a deterministic extractive stand-in, flagged as weaker than a model summary. Stage 2: model summaries, pinned model. | ordinary summarisation | 1, 2 |
| Sum-raw | As Sum, but a summary hit expands to its raw event (the RAW_PROVENANCE move of ledger Entry H). | separates the *index* from the *expansion* | 1, 2 |
| D | Traces under R. At ingest, up to 5 concepts per experience from an extractor independent of the generator's ontology (keyphrases mapped to concept clusters by a fixed local embedding threshold); a trace is concept ids, day and a pointer to the raw event. At retrieval, the concepts of the cue and the active goal activate matching traces, with one-hop spreading through concept co-occurrence under a fixed cap; the pointed-to raw events are fetched into C. Every retrieval logs which concept link caused it. | the mechanism under test | 1 |
| O, S | Controls on D: O uses the generator's own concept labels (upper bound); S shuffles trace-to-event pointers (lower bound). | bounds | 1 |

**Metrics.** Primary: *gold-in-context* for the delayed target at C = 1,000 tokens and R = 10% on M2 probes, arm D against the best of Del, Sum and Sum-raw (chosen on development seeds only). Secondary: D against the references F1 and F2; the same at the other C and R and at M0 and M1 (the dose-response: an honest trace effect should grow as overlap falls); decoy intrusion; **false-association rate** (retrieved events that the gold labels call irrelevant, split by whether a concept link caused them); tokens and wall-clock per retrieval; cost of the write-time and maintenance work, counted separately. Scoring is mechanical (gold event id in context); no judge.

**Decision rules (fixed before Stage 1).** Practical threshold: D exceeds the best of Del, Sum and Sum-raw by at least 0.15 absolute with a paired-bootstrap 95% interval (resampling scenarios) whose lower bound is above zero. If Sum-raw is within 0.05 of D, the reported conclusion is "a summary index with expansion is as good". If F2 (dense over everything) is at or above D, the trace did not beat conventional retrieval and can at most claim a smaller index; say so as a V-H5 result. Report O and S so a ceiling or floor effect is visible. Exploratory tier, no independent review.

**Relevance types (added after the 2026-11-01 discussion).** Lexical overlap is not the only axis. Cross it with *semantic similarity* between cue and gold event (high or low). A topical but lexically disjoint link (an article about sleep, later a goal about memory systems) is expected to be won by dense retrieval, so those cells check the generator rather than carry the claim. The cell where a trace could matter is relevance by **role**: low lexical and low semantic similarity, but the old event encodes a constraint or a failed approach that applies to the new goal (a supplier that delayed shipments, relevant when planning an unrelated launch). That is also where false associations are most likely. Report every arm per cell.

**Negative transfer.** Each cue has decoys that resemble the old experience on the surface but where it must *not* be applied. Report, per arm, how often the context contains a decoy and misses the gold event; this is the measurable form of "should the agent trust this memory".

**Signal validity, Stage 1b ($0).** The generator knows each event's future usefulness, so predicted importance can be compared with measured usefulness directly. Test which write-time signals predict it, against recency and length as baselines: novelty (distance to existing memory), surprise (mismatch with a stated or locally modelled expectation), consequence weight. Novelty, surprise and consequence are different quantities and are kept separate. This does not adapt retention, so it stays inside the observation-only rule of the future-utility protocol, and it is the first honest test of what "importance" means here.

## 4. Stages and gates

1. **Stage 0, construct checks on development seeds ($0).** Proceed only if: the best of Del, Sum and Sum-raw at M2, C = 1,000 tokens and R = 10% lies between 0.2 and 0.8 (headroom, and F2 still shows the information is reachable); measured cue-to-gold overlap matches the three levels; decoys exist; the reveal is identical with and without the history (the existing leakage-test pattern); O reaches at least 0.95 and S sits near chance. Added after the first run: a feasibility gate, so that the best case for D (the oracle on D's own store, Oc, minus the best comparator) can clear the practical threshold at the primary cell; otherwise the cell cannot confirm anything. This is the step that found the length confound in corpus H1.
2. **Stage 1, retrieval-level run on the frozen test seeds ($0).** Model-free, local embeddings.
3. **Stage 2, answer-level run (paid, optional).** A fixed reader (`scripts/arena/fixed_reader.py`) over a sample of arms (A, F2, Del, Sum-raw, D), adding model summaries for Sum and Sum-raw, under a separate small ledger and the existing spend-cap machinery. Only to confirm or qualify a Stage 1 result, or to see whether a null survives at the answer level.

## 5. Cost, effort and risk (INFERENCE unless stated)

- **Cost.** Stages 0 and 1: $0 (local BM25, local embeddings). Stage 2 as written for E0 (not run): 240 test probes x 5 arms (A, F2, Del, Sum-raw, D) x 1 draw = 1,200 reader calls at the measured $0.00046 to $0.00099 per call, about $0.55 to $1.19, with no write-time model calls because the arms are deterministic (INFERENCE; the figure of $1.5 to $4 in the first draft was computed for a different design and did not add up); a pilot of a few cents first (measured precedent: $0.021 found five harness defects). Prices are DeepSeek's; another model costs more. The V3 campaign budget is frozen and gated on a different request, so Stage 2 needs its own authorisation.
- **Effort.** Generator family and its construction test: 1 to 2 engineer-days. Arms behind one interface, harness, statistics: 2 to 3 days. Manual audit of about 30 retrieved associations for the false-association label: about 2 hours. No human review of answers (mechanical scoring). These are estimates.
- **Risks and mitigations.** *Generator artefact*: a templated corpus measures the generator. Mitigate with the independent extractor, the oracle and shuffled controls, the overlap dose-response, and a follow-up on real text (the repository's own paper catalogue as a reading log) before any claim beyond synthetic data. *Circularity*: never reuse the generator's ontology inside arm D. *Headroom*: Stage 0 can stop the study cheaply. *Strong baseline*: F2 or Sum-raw may simply win; that is a result. *Scope creep*: dynamic importance (V-H2), transfer and persistence are explicitly not in this study. *Cost creep*: Stage 2 is separate and capped.

## 6. Doctrine check and decisions needed

Against the [operating doctrine](../00-project/operating-doctrine.md): (1) the answer is partly known, so the labelled contribution is the regime, not the mechanism; (2) falsification value is high, since a null removes a plausible direction; (3) Stages 0 and 1 are model-free and cost nothing, but they build a fresh corpus, which the selection table classes as Tier C (it needs an I1 sealed split); run unsealed, the study is exploratory and claims no tier; (4) the new family and the arm interface might serve some blocked experiments, which is a question, not a claim. `memory/CURRENT_STATE.md` still says new preregistrations are paused until the unexecuted drafts are triaged, but `preregistration-triage-v1.md` is that triage (1 execute now, 16 blocked on corpus and budget, 4 blocked on independent review, 0 retired). Its standing rule is "Do not add a new preregistration until at least one family has an executed result", and `PMLAB-REV-V0-001`, the one "execute now" item, is completed-exploratory-threshold-met; whether that satisfies the rule is the owner's judgement. The doctrine also limits designed-not-executed experiments per track. This document is a proposal, not a preregistration, and E0 was run unregistered.

Decisions for the owner: (a) whether `PMLAB-REV-V0-001` satisfies the standing rule of triage-v1, or an explicit exception is needed, if E0 or a successor is ever to be registered; (b) Stage 2 is not planned after the Stage 1 null; only if the owner wants the answer-level check, authorise a separate small ledger (about $0.55 to $1.19 at the measured prices); (c) whether a future family should aim to unblock the existing blocked-corpus experiments (triage-v1 defines their shared needs as a temporal corpus with supersessions and a multi-cycle compaction corpus, which a delayed-relevance generator does not provide, so the overlap is probably small); (d) accept arm D as *one mechanism under test*, not an architecture, which is what the doctrine requires; (e) whether the Phase 0 of the NIE audit (a read-only snapshot, importer and data-quality report; no spend, no change to that agent, no experiment registered) may proceed as instrument-building independent of the pause; (f) that any in-agent capture hook is the owner's decision about the production agent, outside this project; (g) a separate ledger and a separate registration for any paid run on that agent (the audit estimates $31 to $52, against the frozen V3 cap of $10).

## 7. What would change the plan

Stage 0 fails the headroom check (redesign the corpus, not the arms); the tokenizer choice moves results (report both); F2 or Sum-raw matches D (stop and report the null; publication is the owner's decision; only then consider whether dynamic importance is worth testing); a Stage 1 effect appears only at M0 (it is a lexical-overlap effect, not association); new prior art shows the regime is already measured (cite it and re-scope).

## 8. After the first run (candidates; none registered, authorised or started)

1. **Signal validity** (the Stage 1b of section 3, not built): which write-time signals predict later relevance, on events the experimenter did not author, with recency and length as baselines; novelty, surprise and consequence kept separate; local token probabilities as a cheap surprise measure.
2. **A repeat with traces written by a model**: with a feasibility gate, importance that is informative but noisy, more worlds, and fresh test seeds (100-119 are spent for the E0 configuration).
3. **An optional real-text stage** on a read-only snapshot of a real agent's log, only as a descriptive check: at least 30 natural low-priority-then-used pairs, a reveal horizon of at most 28 days, no spend, and no data derived from third parties may leave the laboratory or enter this repository.
